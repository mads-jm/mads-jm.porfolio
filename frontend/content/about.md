# About Me

I came to software the long way around: years managing retail teams, then a B.S. in Computer Science from Bellevue College, then a full-time move into building things.

Years behind coffee bars and running theatre floors taught me to start with people rather than the org chart, and I build software the same way. Work out what someone actually needs before deciding what to ship. The work I like best sits between the technical detail and the person on the other end of it.

These days it's Python and TypeScript at work and Rust on my own time: terminal UIs, local-first tools, and the systems underneath. The photos below are a few peeks into life outside the terminal.
