Platforms change their terms, get acquired, or quietly die. Anything I write that lives
*only* on someone else's server is on loan.

Markdown in a git repo is the opposite of that. It's mine, it's portable, it outlives any
particular renderer, and I can grep it. The site is just one view over the files.

That's the whole reason this garden is built the way it is — see [[Planting a digital garden]].
